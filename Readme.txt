🕹️ Flappy World Evolution
A High-Stakes Physics-Based Arcade Game

Flappy World Evolution is a modern take on the classic bird-flap genre, built with pure JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. It features dynamic difficulty scaling, a multi-life system, and parallax world rendering.

Key Features:

Dynamic Difficulty: Game speed increases every 10 points to keep players challenged.

Life System: Earn extra lives as you progress to achieve higher scores.

Responsive Design: Fully playable on Desktop (Space/Click) and Mobile (Touch).

Smooth Scenery: Hand-coded parallax mountains and clouds for an immersive world.

How to Play:

Avoid the pipes.

Tap or press Space to flap.

Collect 10 points to speed up and gain a life!
